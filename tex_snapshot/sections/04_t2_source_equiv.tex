\section{Source Equivalence: Undecidable on the Richest Public Arms}
\label{sec:t2}

As implemented, the \citet{tencent2026} floor is single-source
(Section~\ref{sec:setup}), so its use as the significance threshold prices all-source noise from
one source. Because \citet{tencent2026}'s per-seed evaluations are not public, what we audit is
the \emph{practice} --- a single-source floor read as an all-source threshold --- on the richest
public arms with both sources present (S\&N's 10 init-only and 9 data-order-only 1B runs); the
verdict binds the practice wherever it is used. We assess equivalence against a
$[0.5, 2]$ band on $\sigma_{\mathrm{init}}/\sigma_{\mathrm{order}}$ with F and bootstrap
intervals (per-task table: Appendix~\ref{app:t2}).

The median ratio is $0.79$ on primary tasks and $1.17$ on bpb (point estimates, exploratory
only: Appendix~\ref{app:t2}). Undecidability here is structural, not incidental: not one of
the 27 tasks has a CI inside the $2\times$ band under the analytic F intervals ($0/9$ primary,
$0/18$ bpb; $2/27$ under bootstrap) --- a precision statement the degrees of freedom force, not
evidence about the ratio (even at $n = 10$ per arm the interval spans $4.03\times$;
Table~\ref{tab:t2power}; the F intervals are computed on the relative-SD ratio, where the pivot
is approximate; on the raw variance ratio they are exact --- the containment count is $0/27$
under both). The structural point is separate: writing a run's final score as
$f(i,d) = \mu + a(i) + b(d) + c(i,d)$, the init arm identifies the law of $\mu + a(I)$, the
order arm that of $\mu + b(D)$, and the interaction $c$ lives off both arm lines at \emph{any}
arm size (proofs: Appendix~\ref{app:t2-proof}). The two statements below price this classical
variance-decomposition fact at the public arm sizes --- identifiability plus
degrees-of-freedom arithmetic, stated because it bounds what single-source floors can
mean, not because it is new.

\begin{remark}[Non-identifiability of the all-source floor]
\label{prop:t2-nonid}
Let the two arm laws have variances $\sigma_i^2, \sigma_o^2 > 0$. Then
\emph{(i) observational equivalence}: data-generating processes with identical arm laws realize
all-source variances $\sigma_{\mathrm{all}}^2$ of $\sigma_i^2$ \emph{(the init-only floor is
exact)} and $\sigma_i^2 + \sigma_o^2$ \emph{(independent additivity)} alike.
\emph{(ii) one-sided under no cancellation}: if the interaction covaries with neither main
effect, $\rho = \sigma_{\mathrm{all}}/\sigma_i \ge \sqrt{1 + \sigma_o^2/\sigma_i^2}$, and that
no-cancellation premise is itself untestable from these arms.
\end{remark}

\begin{corollary}[No single-source certificate at any public arm size]
\label{cor:t2-2727}
For every one of the $27$ tasks the arm laws alone are compatible with $\rho = 1$ \emph{(the
floor is exact)} and with independent additivity alike; no single-source data exclude either.
\end{corollary}

Read under the bundled semantics of \citet{tencent2026}'s \S3.3 instead, the question becomes
whether the interaction term matters; the structural answer is unchanged
(Remark~\ref{prop:t2-nonid}), and the empirical check would need a bundled arm the public
record lacks.

\paragraph{Verdict.}
\label{sec:t2-verdict}
\textbf{Verdict: undecidable on public evidence, by construction.} This binds the
\emph{practice} --- a single-source floor read as an all-source threshold --- not
\citet{tencent2026}'s own implementation, whose per-seed data are not public. The two sources
are of comparable magnitude at 1B, so an init-only floor is not orders of magnitude off; under
independent additivity the understatement factor is $1.3$--$1.6$ (a model-based sensitivity
estimate; the available cross-check is compatible but cannot confirm it). \textbf{The headline
is the design consequence}: what identifies the all-source floor is a bundled arm or a crossed
init$\times$order design (Appendix~\ref{app:crossed}), not more single-source runs.
