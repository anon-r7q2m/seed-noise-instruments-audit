\section{Source Equivalence: Undecidable on the Richest Public Arms}
\label{sec:t2}

As implemented, the \citet{tencent2026} floor is single-source
(Section~\ref{sec:setup}), so its use as the significance threshold prices all-source noise from
one source. The auditable core is a source-equivalence question: does the across-run spread of
init-only re-runs match that of data-order-only re-runs, and does either approximate the two
combined? Signal \& Noise's released arms (10 init-only, 9 data-order-only 1B runs, one recipe)
are the richest public asset with both arms present (our asset census found no second).
Because \citet{tencent2026}'s per-seed evaluations are not public, what we audit is the
\emph{practice} --- a single-source floor read as an all-source threshold --- instantiated on
the richest arms that exist; the verdict binds any such floor, theirs included as implemented.
We assess
equivalence against a $[0.5, 2]$ band on $\sigma_{\mathrm{init}}/\sigma_{\mathrm{order}}$ with F
and bootstrap intervals (protocol and per-task table: Appendix~\ref{app:t2},
Table~\ref{tab:t2}, and Figure~\ref{fig:t2} in Appendix~\ref{app:t2}), reporting band containment and never reading ``not significant'' as
``equivalent''. The factor-two margin is a convention and we treat it as one: it encodes
``same order of magnitude'' for an SD ratio, and the verdict below does not depend on the
choice -- at every margin up to $2\times$, $0/27$ intervals fit under analytic, bootstrap,
and Bonferroni-widened intervals alike, and containment begins only at $2.5\times$
(Appendix~\ref{app:t2}).
Read under the bundled semantics of \citet{tencent2026}'s \S3.3 instead, the question becomes
whether the interaction term matters --- and the structural answer is unchanged: it lives off
both single-source arms at any budget (Remark~\ref{prop:t2-nonid}), and the empirical check
would need a bundled arm on the same recipe, which the public record lacks
(Section~\ref{sec:t2-sensitivity}).

\paragraph{Point estimates: two sources of comparable magnitude.}
\label{sec:t2-point}
The two sources are of the same order: the median ratio is $0.79$ on
primary tasks and $1.17$ on bpb. Two of the 27 per-task F intervals exclude
1, in opposite directions (HellaSwag $0.35$ [$0.17$, $0.70$]; Winogrande $2.36$ [$1.13$,
$4.79$]); under the global null $\sigma_{\mathrm{init}} = \sigma_{\mathrm{order}}$ one expects
${\approx}1.4$ exclusions by chance alone, neither task survives Bonferroni control over the
27 readouts, and HellaSwag's bootstrap interval includes 1
($[0.15, 1.26]$; Table~\ref{tab:t2}). The 27 readouts are not independent either (8 tasks
appear in both the accuracy and bpb panels), so the effective number of tests is smaller
still. We therefore report the point estimates as exploratory hypotheses, not findings; what
is established is structural (Remark~\ref{prop:t2-nonid}): single-source arms cannot
identify whether an init-only floor understates or overstates the \emph{all-source} noise on
any task.

\paragraph{Undecidability is structural, not incidental.}
\label{sec:t2-power}
This section argues two distinct points, and they should not be conflated: whether
$\sigma_{\mathrm{init}}/\sigma_{\mathrm{order}}$ falls within an equivalence band on the public
arms is a \emph{precision} question, while whether single-source arms can identify the
all-source floor \emph{at all} is a \emph{structural} one, settled by
Remark~\ref{prop:t2-nonid} alone. On the precision question:
not one of the 27 tasks has a CI inside the $2\times$ band ($0/9$ primary, $0/18$ bpb). This is
not a deficiency of these particular runs: a variance-ratio CI's width is a pure function of
degrees of freedom, and even at $n = 10$ per arm it spans $4.03\times$, wider than the $4\times$
band; the S\&N arms (df 9/8) are already the best the public record offers, and pricing parity
needs $n \ge 31$ per arm (Table~\ref{tab:t2power}).
\textbf{Source equivalence at 1B is undecidable on existing evidence.}

Power, however, prices the wrong question: the floor is \emph{used} as a stand-in
for all-source noise, and that is not a power problem at all. Write a run's final score as
$f(i,d) = \mu + a(i) + b(d) + c(i,d)$, with init draw $I$ and
data-order draw $D$ independent by design. The init arm identifies the law of
$\mu + a(I)$ and the order arm that of $\mu + b(D)$; the interaction $c$ lives off both arm
lines and is unconstrained by single-source data at \emph{any} arm size.

\begin{remark}[Non-identifiability of the all-source floor]
\label{prop:t2-nonid}
Let the two arm laws have variances $\sigma_i^2, \sigma_o^2 > 0$. Then
\emph{(i) observational equivalence}: data-generating processes with identical arm laws realize
all-source variances $\sigma_{\mathrm{all}}^2$ of $\sigma_i^2$ \emph{(the init-only floor is
exact)} and $\sigma_i^2 + \sigma_o^2$ \emph{(independent additivity)} alike.
\emph{(ii) one-sided under no cancellation}: if the interaction covaries with neither main
effect, $\rho = \sigma_{\mathrm{all}}/\sigma_i \ge \sqrt{1 + \sigma_o^2/\sigma_i^2}$, and that
no-cancellation premise is itself untestable from these arms. (Sharp but vacuous bounds on
$\sigma_{\mathrm{all}}^2$ from the score range: Appendix~\ref{app:t2-proof}.)
\end{remark}

\begin{corollary}[Undecidable at any arm size]
\label{cor:t2-2727}
For every one of the $27$ tasks the arm laws alone are compatible with $\rho = 1$ \emph{(the
floor is exact)} and with independent additivity alike; no single-source data exclude either.
\end{corollary}

Proofs, with an explicit binary example realizing ratios $1$, $2$, or $9$:
Appendix~\ref{app:t2-proof}. Two statements should not be conflated. The $0/27$ of
Table~\ref{tab:t2} is a \emph{precision} statement about the richest public arms: at their
degrees of freedom no conventional equivalence band is certifiable ($0/27$ at every band up to
$2\times$ under analytic, bootstrap, and Bonferroni-widened intervals alike; containment begins
only at $2.5\times$). The \emph{structural} statement --- that no enlargement of single-source
arms can identify the all-source floor --- is carried entirely by
Remark~\ref{prop:t2-nonid} and its Corollary, not by the $0/27$ count.
What identifies $\sigma_{\mathrm{all}}$ is a design
change: a bundled arm or a crossed init$\times$order design \citep{dodge2020finetuning}.

\paragraph{How much does an init-only floor understate all-source noise? A sensitivity result.}
\label{sec:t2-sensitivity}
A natural repair inflates the init-only floor by
$\sqrt{\sigma_{\mathrm{init}}^2 + \sigma_{\mathrm{order}}^2}/\sigma_{\mathrm{init}}$: median
$1.62$ on primary tasks, $1.32$ on bpb. This is a
\textbf{model-based sensitivity estimate} assuming independent addition with no interaction,
which these arms do not contain and cannot test
(Remark~\ref{prop:t2-nonid}(ii)). Our own empirical cross-check lands lower but is
underpowered: DataDecide's 1B bundled-seed noise (a different corpus and architecture;
Appendix~\ref{app:t2}) sits at a median $1.06\times$ the S\&N init-only $\sigma$ ($1.19\times$
after $c_4$ correction), its own 95\% interval spanning ${\sim}6\times$ at df $= 2$
(Table~\ref{tab:t2bundle}). The defensible statement: \emph{under additivity the understatement
factor is $1.3$--$1.6$; the available check is compatible but cannot confirm it}.
What the point estimates do not establish is per-task dominance (Section~\ref{sec:t2-point});
what removes the single-source floor as a task-level safe surrogate is structural
(Remark~\ref{prop:t2-nonid}).

\paragraph{Verdict.}
\label{sec:t2-verdict}
\textbf{Verdict: undecidable on public evidence, by construction.} The two sources are of
comparable magnitude at 1B, so an init-only floor is not orders of magnitude off; but
equivalence itself is undecidable on the richest public arms ($0/27$ tasks in band),
per-task dominance is not resolvable at available power, and the quantitative inflation
factor is a sensitivity estimate whose cross-check cannot confirm it.
\textbf{The headline is the design consequence}: the obstacle is identifiability, not
power; what identifies the all-source floor is a bundled arm
or a crossed init$\times$order design (Corollary~\ref{cor:t2-2727}).
