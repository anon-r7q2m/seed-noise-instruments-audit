\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

Run-to-run variability is well documented \citep{nondeterminism2021, latentstate2023,
picard2021seed}; whether a single source can stand in for total variance task-by-task is
undecidable on public arms (Section~\ref{sec:t2}), and \citet{dodge2020finetuning}'s crossed
init$\times$order design is what our assets lack. Where that line measures instability, we audit
the instruments built to \emph{price} it \citep{bouthillier2021variance, dehghani2021lottery};
\citet{miller2024errorbars} argue for error bars on LLM evaluations,
\citet{madaan2024variance} quantify that variance across benchmarks, and
\citet{bowyer2025clt} show the small-$n$ statistics those bars rest on fail below a few
hundred datapoints; we supply the magnitudes and regime boundaries such error bars need.
\citet{nityasya2023debt} call the cost of under-measured claims \emph{scientific debt};
rliable \citep{agarwal2021rliable} is our closest methodological kin.
\citet{wortsman2023smallscale} validate small-scale proxies for training instabilities and
\citet{choshen2024hitchhiker} systematize scaling-law estimation;
\citet{tencent2026} revisits \citet{narang2021transfer}; \citet{zhou2026repetition} isolate
one driver of the 4M inversion (repetition rates shift with budget). Our conclusions bear most
directly on data-mixing proxy methods \citep{ye2024datamixing, liu2024regmix}.
