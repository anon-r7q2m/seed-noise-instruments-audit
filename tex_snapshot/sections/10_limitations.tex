\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}

Four bounds on scope. (i) \textbf{The audit is re-analysis on one panel; the controls are
small}: the headline transfer results live on DataDecide's 25 recipes (one training stack,
one evaluation suite), and Appendix~\ref{app:crossed}'s self-run controls
are deliberately small (20M, one recipe family); conclusions inherit those
regimes.
(ii) \textbf{Source decomposition exists at few points}: S\&N's 1B release, PolyPythias's 160M release, and our 20M crossed grid
(Appendix~\ref{app:crossed}).
(iii) \textbf{Thin cells, model-based attribution}: atlas cells have
$n = 3$ (df $= 2$); we publish across-cell quantiles, not per-cell intervals, and the $13.5\%$
[$8.3$, $57.0$] attribution is the fitted model's (Table~\ref{tab:hme}), stable across model-ingredient variants and leave-one-recipe-out (Appendix~\ref{app:t3-hme}). (iv) \textbf{We judge detectability, not truth}: an audited paper's conclusions are out of scope.
\emph{Analysis freedom}: the three audits' estimands and the assets' defect inventory were
fixed before the analysis; the separability-SNR readout, the 4M inversion, and the 60M--90M
jump were found in the data and are reported as exploratory findings with resampling intervals, not prespecified hypotheses. \textbf{Closing.} An instrument that reproduces
where it was calibrated is not thereby calibrated where it is used; the remedies are
Section~\ref{sec:prescriptions}'s norms.
